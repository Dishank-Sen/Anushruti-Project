# Indian Sign Language references

Maths, science and Sign Studio share `lib/isl-sources.ts`. It contains English dictionary terms, exact original filenames and file IDs from the **Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC), Government of India**. Source links checked on 21 September 2026.

- [ISLRTC dictionary source page](https://islrtc.nic.in/isl-dictionary/) links the [original public dictionary folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1U-Pr4r1-cupgNOOq9NH_uTsQnPSVEKco). The registry was checked against the filenames in its alphabet and number folders.
- [ISLRTC document catalogue](https://islrtc.nic.in/documents/page/10/) publishes **Alphabet in ISL** and **Numbers in ISL**. `ISL_CHARTS` links the original government-hosted PDFs and the demonstrations printed on those charts.
- The [publisher's copyright policy](https://islrtc.nic.in/copyright-policy/) does not grant unrestricted reproduction. We link to or embed original hosted resources with acknowledgement; we do not copy, crop, redraw or redistribute these figures/videos. They are not covered by this repository's code licence.

Videos load only after the learner selects **Watch**. The selected English term stays visible as a caption; ISLRTC's dictionary videos also display their English terms. Both videos and charts have direct source links if an embed fails or the device cannot display PDFs. External hosts need internet access and may receive the usual connection metadata. No microphone or learner recordings are sent to these hosts.

These are **dictionary references, not automatic sentence translation**. ISL has its own grammar, movement and regional variants. Unknown words are explicitly unlinked; we never generate a substitute gesture or pretend that an alphabet shape is a number. The previous unsourced hand drawings, movement descriptions and stop-word-based “translation” have been removed. English Fitzgerald reading colours remain separate from ISL.

Every science module has contextual terms in its typed `islTerms` field. Maths retains its existing chapter vocabulary and arithmetic activities. To add a reference, locate the exact intended word/sense through ISLRTC's source page, record its original filename and ID, and request qualified ISL/deaf-educator review for instructional suitability before merging. File provenance does not itself establish age suitability or linguistic equivalence in every sentence. Do not infer a sign from an English synonym or a generic hand icon.

The **ISL support** setting hides in-lesson reference panels; Sign Studio remains available as an intentional destination. All learning activities still work without sound, speech or external media.
